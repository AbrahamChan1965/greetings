Dear friend, Happy Thanksgiving.

More than four hundred years ago, on a bay at Plymouth, a long table stood in the autumn light. Pilgrims and Wampanoag sat together around it, bowing their heads in thanks for the corn, the pumpkins, and the wild fowl they had just harvested. The food was not plentiful, yet it was enough to teach a people who had survived a terrible winter, and lost nearly everything, one thing again: grace is never to be taken for granted.

The Bible says: every good gift and every perfect gift is from above. Like manna, food, friendship, and peace are not things we earn; they are gifts that descend gently from heaven.

May this grace from above fall on you and your family too, and thank you for walking with me, my family, and the Golden Lampstand Ministry on the journey of ministry. Happy Thanksgiving. Rev. Dr. Abraham Chan, 11.26.2026.
