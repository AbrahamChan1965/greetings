Autumn light on Plymouth Bay,
The bay listens to the waves.
Corn and pumpkin fill the board,
Wild game and seafood, thanks to the Lord.
After the harvest came the winter cold,
Our lives were not our own.
Every good gift from above,
Like manna falling as snow.
The Pilgrim and the Native son
Sit down as one at the board.
Different tongues, they embrace as one,
Thankful prayer becomes a song.
The Lord's grace falls on all,
Family and friends are blessed.
Counting mercies, hearts at rest,
Thanksgiving crowns it all.
