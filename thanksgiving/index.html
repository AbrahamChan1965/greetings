1621 普利茅斯的秋光與感恩 Autumn Light & Gratitude Since 1621

在這個充滿即時訊息與數位喧囂的時代，我想為你留下一片安靜的秋日長桌。四百多年前的秋天，經歷嚴冬與試煉的人們在普利茅斯海灣旁圍坐，為著玉米、野禽、清水與彼此的存留獻上感謝。

Amid the rush of constant notifications and digital noise, I wanted to set aside a quiet autumn table for you. Over four centuries ago along Plymouth Bay, those who had weathered a harsh winter gathered around simple harvest fare to give thanks for corn, wildfowl, clean water, and the gift of life shared together.

願那些從上頭緩緩降下的恩典，在繁忙歲月中重新安頓你的心靈；願你在家人、朋友與呼召之中，看見真實而長存的豐盛。

May the quiet mercies descending from above restore stillness to your soul, and may you behold lasting abundance among family, friends, and your calling this Thanksgiving.

歷史考據註記 · Historical Note

1621 年秋收慶典記載於 Edward Winslow《Mourt's Relation》與 William Bradford《Of Plymouth Plantation》，當年的主要收穫包含印第安玉米（Indian Corn）、南瓜（Pumpkins）、野火雞與水禽（Wildfowl）、鱈魚與鱸魚（Cod & Bass）。

Documented in Edward Winslow's Mourt's Relation and William Bradford's Of Plymouth Plantation, the 1621 Plymouth harvest centered on Indian corn, pumpkins, wildfowl, cod, and bass—shared across three days of gratitude.
