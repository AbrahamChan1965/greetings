PLYMOUTH · AUTUMN 1621

「各樣美善的恩賜和各樣全備的賞賜，都是從上頭來的。」“Every good gift and every perfect gift is from above.”雅各書 1:17James 1:17

1621 普利茅斯的秋光與感恩Autumn Light & Gratitude Since 1621

親愛的朋友，感恩節快樂。Dear friend, Happy Thanksgiving.

四百多年前，在普利茅斯的海灣邊，一張長桌擺在秋光裡。清教徒和萬帕諾亞格人圍坐在一起，為剛收穫的玉米、南瓜和野禽，低頭謝恩。桌上的食物不多，卻足夠讓一群歷經嚴冬、幾乎失去一切的人，重新學會一件事：恩典不是理所當然的。More than four hundred years ago, on a bay at Plymouth, a long table stood in the autumn light. Pilgrims and Wampanoag sat together around it, bowing their heads in thanks for the corn, the pumpkins, and the wild fowl they had just harvested. The food was not plentiful, yet it was enough to teach a people who had survived a terrible winter, and lost nearly everything, one thing again: grace is never to be taken for granted.

聖經說：各樣美善的恩賜和各樣全備的賞賜，都是從上頭來的。就像嗎哪一樣，食物、友誼與平安，都不是我們掙來的，而是從天上緩緩降下的禮物。The Bible says: every good gift and every perfect gift is from above. Like manna, food, friendship, and peace are not things we earn; they are gifts that descend gently from heaven.

願這一份從上頭來的恩典，也臨到你和你的家人，謝謝你與我、我的一家和基督燈台事工在事奉的路上同行。感恩節快樂，陳曉東牧師 11.26.2026。May this grace from above fall on you and your family too, and thank you for walking with me, my family, and the Golden Lampstand Ministry on the journey of ministry. Happy Thanksgiving. Rev. Dr. Abraham Chan, 11.26.2026.
