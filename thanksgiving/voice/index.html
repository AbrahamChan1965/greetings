一張安靜的卡片，送給你A quiet card, for you

來自陳曉東牧師的語音問候A voice greeting from Rev. Dr. Abraham Chan

陳曉東牧師親自錄音Recorded by Rev. Dr. Abraham Chan

在一個安靜的秋日，一張長桌擺在普利茅斯的海灣邊。清教徒與萬帕諾亞格人圍坐在一起，為著剛剛收穫的玉米、南瓜與野禽，低頭謝恩。桌上的食物不多，卻足夠讓一群歷經嚴冬、幾乎失去一切的人，重新學會一件事：恩典不是理所當然的。On a quiet autumn day, a long table stood by the bay at Plymouth. Pilgrims and Wampanoag sat together around it, bowing their heads in thanks for the corn, the pumpkins, and the wild fowl they had just harvested. The food was not plentiful, yet it was enough to teach a people who had survived a terrible winter, and lost nearly everything, one thing again: grace is never to be taken for granted.

這張卡片所記念的，正是那一刻——恩典從上頭來。雅各書說：「各樣美善的恩賜和各樣全備的賞賜都是從上頭來的。」就像嗎哪一樣，食物、友誼與平安，都不是我們掙來的，而是從天上緩緩降下的禮物。This card remembers that moment — the moment grace came down from above. As James writes, “Every good gift and every perfect gift is from above.” Like manna, food, friendship, and peace are not things we earn; they are gifts that descend gently from heaven.

感恩詩歌Thanksgiving Poems

秋光灑在普利茅斯，
海灣靜靜聽禱聲。
玉米南瓜擺滿桌，
鹿肉魚鮮謝主恩。
嚴冬已過春又來，
存留性命非偶然。
各樣美善從上頭，
嗎哪降下似甘霖。
清教徒與原住民，
圍桌同坐一家親。
不同言語同一心，
感恩化作歌聲新。
願這恩典臨我家，
親人朋友同蒙福。
數算主恩心安息，
年年秋天唱感恩。

Autumn light on Plymouth Bay,
The tide keeps time with prayer.
Corn and pumpkin fill the board,
Venison and fish are there.
The winter passed, the spring returned,
Our lives were not our own.
Every good gift from above,
Like manna gently sown.
The Pilgrim and the Native son
Sit down as one at the board.
Different tongues, one thankful heart,
Our gratitude to the Lord.
May this grace fall on my home,
On family, friend, and call.
Counting mercies, hearts at rest,
Thanksgiving crowns it all.
